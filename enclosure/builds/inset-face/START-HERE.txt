INSET FACE FIT / RETENTION
The face perimeter is diameter 41 mm in a diameter 41.6 mm opening: 0.3 mm radial clearance. Its flange is diameter 43.2 mm in a diameter 43.8 mm rear pocket: also 0.3 mm radial clearance. Do not scale the whole model to tune these fits.
Insert the face from inside the hood. Its flange catches the integral front ledge and prevents it passing out through the front. Three support posts on the internal retainer and three soft insulating pads capture it from behind when the hood is installed. No front screws or glue are required by this design.
Additional pads: three diameter 2.4 mm discs, nominal 0.5 mm WORKING thickness, at x/y (-18,-9), (18,-9), (0,20). Actual material/preload and print tolerances need testing. Do not force the hood down against pads that are too thick. These pads are hardware references, NOT rigid STL parts.
Hold the face when opening the hood: with the hood removed it can withdraw rearward for service. The independently screwed internal carriers remain retained.
