PRINTING / FINISHES
PETG is a fit-test starting material, not a validated profile. Inspect roofs, fine surface treatments, eyes, carrier restraints and support-removal access in layer preview. Keep acoustic/USB/button openings, screw pilots, guide hole and band slots clear. Optical guide should be translucent or a suitable rod, not metallic. No G-code supplied.
Metallic-looking CAD colours do not specify metal construction. Use nonconductive finishes unless electrical isolation and RF performance are separately validated. Keep paint, clear coats and glue out of ports and moving/retaining interfaces. Fine decoration may need calibrated printing or post-finishing.

EDITABILITY
Native files are editable solid snapshots with hidden profile definitions, not PartDesign feature-history trees. Per-image JSON profiles and the BuildJewelryStudies.FCMacro/JewelryEnvelopeGeometry.py scripts live in Recorder-Enclosures and reuse the existing verified build logic. Sleek_Round/Sleek_Square source documents must remain available. Completed verification files are resume checkpoints; intentionally invalidate a changed design's checkpoint before regeneration. Loading the macro does not build. UI is serviced between operations; a single Boolean/mesh operation can still briefly block. No regeneration is required to inspect finished exports.
