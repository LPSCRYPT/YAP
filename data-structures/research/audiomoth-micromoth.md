# AudioMoth / MicroMoth — data architecture research

Researched: 2026-11-03.

Evidence: public source and documentation inspection only. No hardware, authenticated service, model-quality, security, or runtime validation was performed. Implementation claims describe inspected code, not confirmed production deployment. Branch links may change.

- **Scope and hardware:** https://www.openacousticdevices.info/audiomoth documents a full-spectrum acoustic logger for audible/ultrasonic recording, uncompressed WAV on microSD, 8–384 kHz, configurable USB and onboard UTC clock. AudioMoth is 58×48×15 mm, with 3×AA supply; MicroMoth is a 32×24 mm, 5 g (without battery) miniature AudioMoth 1.1.0 variant, 3.7–6 V external power, compatible with existing firmware/software. This is suitable evidence for a small research capture module—not a finished always-connected speech pendant.
- **Actual stored schema, source verified:** Firmware `main.c` defines RIFF/WAV, one channel, PCM, 16 bits/sample; `LIST/INFO` metadata contains `IART` with AudioMoth serial and `ICMT` with recording date/time plus UTC offset, deployment/device reference, gain, battery voltage, temperature and optional external microphone indication. See header around lines 280–327 and `setHeaderComment` around 540–625: https://github.com/OpenAcousticDevices/AudioMoth-Firmware-Basic/blob/master/src/main.c . These are recording-level provenance/timestamps, **not word/utterance timestamps or speaker schema**.
- **Processing/API:** Source exposes scheduling, digital filter modes, amplitude/frequency-trigger settings and GPS/acoustic time support; this is capture/signal conditioning, not ASR, diarization, entity extraction, summarization or knowledge-graph construction. Local USB integration uses `audiomoth-hid` over USB HID, with Electron configuration/time tools and modifiable firmware. Primary source index: https://www.openacousticdevices.info/open-source ; firmware: https://github.com/OpenAcousticDevices/AudioMoth-Firmware-Basic ; host interface: https://github.com/OpenAcousticDevices/audiomoth-hid . No hosted transcript/export/retrieval API is established by these sources: the portable artifact is the WAV file copied from storage, with downstream analysis supplied separately.
- **Privacy and openness:** Local microSD capture does not require a vendor cloud transcription service. However, do not equate offline WAV with encryption at rest: no such protection is established by the inspected firmware/docs, and physical SD-card access matters. OAD publishes software/firmware under MIT; its hardware policy says older revisions are released under Creative Commons Attribution after newer iterations—not an unconditional claim every latest board design is already open.
