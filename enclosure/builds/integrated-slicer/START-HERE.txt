These are complete enclosure-shell print sets, NOT the standalone rectangular mount coupon.
The recessed receiver is built into each case base. Use either the flush insert or the clip insert in the same case; changing inserts does not require opening the electronics enclosure.

STATUS
Slicer-loadable, geometrically checked FIT-TEST assemblies. They have NOT been physically printed or mechanically validated. SD-holder dimensions, microphone/motor thickness, amplifier and antenna envelopes remain provisional. Internal PCB retention, wiring and acoustic seals are not finalized. The small button remains an intentional placeholder; no functioning button insert is included. Do not treat these files as production-ready or validated complete electronics housings.

IMPORT INTO YOUR SLICER
Import the .stl files from ONE design folder as separate objects, not as overlapping parts of a single assembly. Units are millimeters; scale 100%. Each mesh is placed at Z=0 in its intended starting print orientation. Use Auto Arrange to separate objects on the build plate. Check the selected printer, nozzle, material, bed size and layer preview before slicing.
Assembly.FCStd and Assembly-Alternatives.step are CAD references, not the print layout. The STEP intentionally contains both alternative inserts in their installed positions; do NOT import that STEP as one printable object.

STARTING FDM PRINT APPROACH — NOT A VERIFIED PRINTER PROFILE
PETG is the suggested starting material for fit and flexure testing. A 0.4 mm nozzle with approximately 0.16–0.20 mm layers is a reasonable starting point; verify thin latch walls in your slicer. Use at least 3 perimeters where geometry permits. Small flexures should slice as solid walls, not sparse infill.
Base: cavity upward. The recessed channel faces the bed and requires removable support under its roof/overhangs. Inspect support access carefully; clean support material out of the rails and latch recess before assembly. Do not use unremovable solid support in the sliding interface.
Lid: outside face down, locating lip upward. Inspect screw holes and surface finish.
Both inserts: supplied on their sides. Use a brim if needed. Inspect supports around the clip arch and tiny latch, and avoid fusing support into flexure gaps. These are suggestions, not a claim that all parts are support-free.
Light guide: clear/translucent material; optical performance untested.
No printer-specific G-code or prevalidated slicer profile is supplied.

ASSEMBLY / SWAPPING
1. Before installing electronics, clean the rails and test the insert by hand. Do not force a tight or jammed insert.
2. Slide the insert in from the USB/SD edge, toward the closed end of the recessed track.
3. Check that its side latch engages the recess and resists withdrawal.
4. To remove, press the exposed latch sideways inward (toward the insert center), then slide it back out toward the USB/SD edge.
5. Insert the other module. The flush insert leaves a flat rear face apart from small running clearances and latch access.
6. Fit the light guide in the lid; use a suitable clear adhesive only if needed and keep it out of optical/slide surfaces.
7. Lid uses three M2-size screw positions with 1.6 mm pilot holes and 2.2 mm clearance holes. Select screw type/length against actual print and pilot depth; do not force unsuitable machine screws into plastic or allow tips to contact electronics.
8. Use a breakaway necklace cord. Eyelet strength, clip grip, latch fatigue and impact resistance are untested.

GEOMETRY CHECKS PERFORMED
- All supplied parts valid single CAD solids.
- STL meshes closed, no detected non-manifold geometry or self-intersections.
- Meshes normalized to Z=0, millimeter dimensions.
- No modeled seated insert/base or electronics-shell intersections above 0.001 mm3.
- Original 1.4 mm protective floor under the battery preserved.
- Engaged latch obstructs withdrawal; released kinematic proxy clears sampled withdrawal positions.
These checks are not a substitute for physical fit, force, fatigue, RF or acoustic testing.
