## Whole-body design brief

The user rejected face-only/inlay-led changes and later rejected near-identical chamfer/corner refinements. The durable requirement is small, sleek, distinct **whole-body** silhouettes and three-dimensional sections with retained hardware, flat rear, centered indicator, necklace eye and optional removable clip. A visual comparison must show geometry, not merely changed colours. Material colour is not a construction specification.

## Why further shrinkage is not cosmetic

The battery, amplifier/SD breakouts, microphone seats, motor and independently retained stack set the envelope. Smaller hardware or a new arrangement is a separate engineering change requiring a new packing/retention analysis. Removing real retention, hiding component intersections, narrowing only the render, or shrinking all STLs would not fulfill the design brief.

Jewelry bodies span 22–25.9 mm maximum thickness in the preserved reports, compared with Sleek's 20.8 mm. They pursue reference-specific whole-body character, not minimum volume/thickness across all historical families. The two microphone envelopes are a preserved baseline, not a new user-required microphone count.
