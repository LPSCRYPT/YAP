WHAT CHANGES
01: A sheared four-sided body with one genuinely sloping roof. Thickness varies approximately20.64..26.96 mm across the footprint. No flat face plus decorative bevel: the entire roof is inclined.
02: A50x50 mm lower volume, a smaller46x48.8 mm upper volume shifted(+1,-.2) mm, and a recessed waist band. Upper corners and shoulder are softened. This is one continuous hood with a modeled waist, not an extra detachable mid-case seam. Electronics still service from the rear.
03: A true elongated capsule with circular ends and short straight flanks, sliced diagonally at the upper right through its full height. Narrower but longer than the original theme body.
04: Two broad roof planes meet along a real off-centre diagonal ridge, x+0.4y=2. Edge thickness drops to20.8 mm; ridge reaches26.4 mm. These are exterior folded-looking surfaces, not a sheet-metal fabrication claim.
05: Broad shoulders taper along unequal lower diagonals to a lower end offset4 mm right. Directional silhouette instead of a clipped square.
06: A half-round left flank opposed by a flat right flank and broad diagonal facets. It keeps a50x50 mm footprint while contrasting curved and planar sides.

The wedge and folded concepts deliberately add depth to express their forms. Split Level and Soft Hard Hybrid are the compact shortlist. Sharp/faceted edges are CAD concept geometry: comfort, snagging and edge finishing require a physical fit print. Curved outlines/shoulders use sampled profiles and may show small facets.

VERIFICATION / LIMITS
All six passed valid-single-solid checks; detailed shell/base Boolean checks; component containment; component/hardware/wire-reservation interference checks; eight sampled shell-extraction positions; seven clip-release positions. Carrier screw penetration into base pilots is intentional threaded engagement. Each STL was checked closed/manifold/self-intersection-free before export and reimported closed/manifold. See per-design or root verification.json.
These checks do not establish continuous-motion clearance, manufacturing tolerance stacks, minimum manufactured wall thickness, strength or production readiness. Nominal walls/roofs start at1.4 mm; Split Level uses a thicker upper-cavity offset and an early internal transition to support the external step.
No physical print, fit, bail-load, fatigue, acoustic, optical, RF, thermal or charging tests performed. Ports are untuned; no waterproofing or acoustic isolation claimed.
SD package remains provisional24x18x3 mm in this orientation. Microphone/motor thickness assumed3 mm; amplifier/driver/antenna/connector packages remain provisional. Direct microphone wiring is assumed; tall headers do not fit. Confirm real parts before final fabrication.
PETG is a fit-test starting point, not a verified printer profile. Preview supports beneath roofs, eye and downward carrier restraints, and ensure support removal access. Keep ports, screw pilots and band slots clear. No printer-specific G-code supplied.
