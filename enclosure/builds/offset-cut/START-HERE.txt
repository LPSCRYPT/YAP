01: Tight square footprint with unequal diagonal cuts and a narrow roof chamfer. Strongest compact option.
02: Dominant diagonal cut and a much broader/deeper roof bevel. More pronounced facets, slightly larger than the original: a styling tradeoff, not a size improvement.
03: Wider upper shoulders and narrower lower outline, with unequal diagonal corner cuts. More directional silhouette, smaller than the original.
04: Compact outline with physically softened corners and a rounded shoulder transition. Corner/shoulder curves are sampled CAD approximations, not material softness.
Each changes the full shell AND its matching rear outline. None uses a decorative face insert. These are compact fits around the existing component arrangement, not proven absolute minimum dimensions.

ASSEMBLY / HARDWARE
The retained component layout comes from Sleek_Round; 24.5x24.5x5 mm protected battery, two retained microphone modules, XIAO, SD and supporting electronics. Battery support and locating stops remain, with nominal0.4 mm upper clearance. Never compress the pouch.
Three M2x14 mm countersunk carrier screws into plastic pilots independently retain the stack. Head envelope about diameter4 x1.2 mm. Three M1.6x6 mm countersunk screws close the hood using1.3 mm plastic pilots. Confirm actual screw dimensions/thread suitability; do not overtighten or substitute longer screws.
XIAO/amplifier/driver require thin insulating retaining bands: up to3 mm wide, about0.15 mm thick. Driver band runs along Y, others along X. Thick cable ties do not fit. Nominal0.3 mm insulating pads restrain microphone/speaker rims, motor and connector; nominal0.5 mm pad at SD bridge. Pad only the speaker's rigid rim, not its diaphragm. Antenna requires insulating removable adhesive. These are extra nonprinted restraints; select and verify real materials/preload.
Install lower components/carrier, upper components/carrier, pads/bands and top retainer. Secure carrier screws. Install centered LED/light guide, lower hood over the stack and fasten rear closure screws. SD access is internal; leave wiring service slack. Button opening and switch envelope remain provisional pending the actual actuator.
Necklace eye has3.6 mm bore. Use a breakaway cord; printed bail strength is untested. Body length including eye is56.2,60.27,58.23,56.2 mm respectively.
Optional clip adds12 mm thickness. Receiver uses two M2x8 mm countersunk screws into flush M2 inserts at x=+-14,y=-15, assumed about3.2 mm OD x3 mm long; follow actual insert manufacturer's pilot requirements. Clip slides off tool-free. Removing receiver for a completely flat necklace back needs its two screws removed, without electronics disassembly. Dovetail/latch geometry unchanged: about0.305 mm minimum flank allowance,0.35 mm floor and0.4 mm mouth per side. Print tolerance and latch fatigue need physical testing.
